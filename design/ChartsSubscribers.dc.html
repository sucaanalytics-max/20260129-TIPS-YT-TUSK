These control limits are indicative, not valid
A Shewhart chart assumes roughly normal, independent observations. This series is neither. 12 days breach +2σ and none breach −2σ — a symmetric process would split them evenly — because YouTube rounds every channel to the nearest 1,000, so net adds arrive in lumps rather than continuously. The raw figure swung from −28,053 to +200,000 here, which is measurement granularity, not audience behaviour, and it puts σ at 35,382 against a mean of 15,174. Four days sit beyond the plotted axis and are marked with arrows rather than rescaling everything else into a flat line.

Read the limits as a rough envelope and the moving averages as the real signal: the ladder says growth is decelerating, with the 15-day running 29.9% below the quarterly pace.
