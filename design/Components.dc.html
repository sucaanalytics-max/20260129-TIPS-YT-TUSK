Why exactly three
Under deuteranopia and protanopia hue collapses onto a single blue–yellow axis. Every four-hue set tried failed an all-pairs colour-blindness check — the best candidate put blue and purple ΔE 0.3 apart under protanopia, which is indistinguishable.
Three is a hard ceiling, not a preference. Beyond three series, facet into small multiples or fold the tail into #8B97A8 and label it directly.
TIPS is #0072B2 and SAREGAMA #D55E00 on every board — fixed, never reallocated by series order.

06 Status & badges colour + glyph + word · all three, always
✓ Good ▲ Warning ◆ Serious ✕ Critical i Info — Unknown
✓ Fresh| ▲ Stale 2d| ◆ Backfill running| ✕ Cron failed| — Never run
The same four states, applied to pipeline health. Shapes stay attached to states across every board.
The rule: good-vs-bad is red-vs-green, which is exactly the axis a deuteranope or protanope loses. Colour therefore never carries the state on its own — every badge is colour + a distinct glyph + the word, and the glyph shapes (✓ ▲ ◆ ✕ —) are separable in pure greyscale.
The same rule governs table deltas (▲ / ▼ / —) and chart breaches, which are ringed rather than merely recoloured.
