Scope — the four decisions taken
Settled. Listed here so approval covers them explicitly.
1
Sidebar navigation with a company switcher
Fixed 290px. One company in scope; the switcher is the only global control.
2
Five sections plus one Compare view
Overview · Nowcast · Content Analytics · Anomalies · Data Table. Compare is the only two-company screen.
3
Views vs price indexed to 100, never dual-axis
Two free y-scales can be slid to imply any correlation; one shared axis cannot. Pearson r, n and significance sit beside the chart.
4
Match the reference's density — fix three defects
Colour-blind-unsafe series → the validated three. Colour-only status → glyph plus colour. Absent-as-zero → an em dash.
