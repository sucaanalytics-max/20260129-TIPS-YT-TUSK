THE RULE THAT DECIDES PLACEMENT
A figure sits at Level 0 only if it can be scored against something that actually gets reported.

At Level 1 if it feeds a Level 0 number and its assumptions are visible.

At Level 2 if it is measured but doesn't yet feed anything.

Nowhere, if it can't be checked at all.
